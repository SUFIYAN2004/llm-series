# Architecture notes

One section per model: what it is, where the code is, and any simplification made in this repo.

### 1. N-gram (KN 3-gram)
*Reference: Kneser & Ney 1995*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/ngram.py`](../llmseries/models/ngram.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/01_ngram.ipynb`](../notebooks/01_ngram.ipynb)

Count-based baseline: Kneser-Ney smoothed trigram on BPE tokens. No gradients, no embeddings; shows what pure statistics give and why sparsity motivates neural LMs.

### 2. Neural n-gram (Bengio)
*Reference: Bengio et al. 2003*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/nnlm.py`](../llmseries/models/nnlm.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/02_nnlm.ipynb`](../notebooks/02_nnlm.ipynb)

Embeddings + fixed 8-token window + MLP. Bridge from counting to neural LMs; anything beyond 8 tokens is invisible.

### 3. Vanilla RNN
*Reference: Elman 1990*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/rnn.py`](../llmseries/models/rnn.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/03_rnn.ipynb`](../notebooks/03_rnn.ipynb)

Elman RNN (tanh), 2 layers. Recurrence and BPTT; expect the weakest neural model because of vanishing gradients.

### 4. LSTM
*Reference: Hochreiter & Schmidhuber 1997*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/rnn.py`](../llmseries/models/rnn.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/04_lstm.ipynb`](../notebooks/04_lstm.ipynb)

2-layer LSTM. Gates and a cell state fix the vanishing-gradient problem of the vanilla RNN.

### 5. GRU
*Reference: Cho et al. 2014*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/rnn.py`](../llmseries/models/rnn.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/05_gru.ipynb`](../notebooks/05_gru.ipynb)

2-layer GRU: LSTM with fewer gates (update and reset only).

### 6. GRU + Bahdanau attn
*Reference: Bahdanau et al. 2014*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/bahdanau.py`](../llmseries/models/bahdanau.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/06_bahdanau.ipynb`](../notebooks/06_bahdanau.ipynb)

2-layer GRU plus additive attention over all previous hidden states. Simplification: attention sits on top of the GRU instead of inside the decoder loop. O(T^2) memory.

### 7. GPT (decoder-only)
*Reference: Vaswani et al. 2017; Radford et al. 2019*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/gpt.py`](../llmseries/models/gpt.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/07_gpt.ipynb`](../notebooks/07_gpt.ipynb)

Decoder-only Transformer: LayerNorm, GELU MLP, learned positional embeddings, causal self-attention. The baseline everything is judged against. Learned positions cannot extrapolate past 256 tokens.

### 8. Modern Transformer (RoPE/RMS/SwiGLU/GQA)
*Reference: Su 2021; Zhang & Sennrich 2019; Shazeer 2020; Ainslie 2023*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/modern.py`](../llmseries/models/modern.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/08_modern.ipynb`](../notebooks/08_modern.ipynb)

Transformer with LLaMA-style upgrades: RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, grouped-query attention. Compare directly with GPT.

### 9. Linear attention
*Reference: Katharopoulos et al. 2020*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/linattn.py`](../llmseries/models/linattn.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/09_linattn.ipynb`](../notebooks/09_linattn.ipynb)

Softmax replaced by an elu+1 feature map so attention equals an RNN with fixed-size state. Training uses the parallel masked form (still O(T^2) compute); the recurrent form is what gives O(T) inference.

### 10. Gated DeltaNet
*Reference: Schlag et al. 2021; Yang et al. 2024*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/deltanet.py`](../llmseries/models/deltanet.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/10_deltanet.ipynb`](../notebooks/10_deltanet.ipynb)

Gated delta rule: a matrix-valued state is decayed, then corrected toward each new value (error-driven write). Implemented as a sequential loop, so it is the slowest model to train.

### 11. S4D (SSM)
*Reference: Gu et al. 2021, 2022*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/s4d.py`](../llmseries/models/s4d.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/11_s4d.ipynb`](../notebooks/11_s4d.ipynb)

Diagonal state-space model run in convolution mode via FFT. Linear time-invariant: the kernel does not depend on the input.

### 12. Hyena
*Reference: Poli et al. 2023*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/hyena.py`](../llmseries/models/hyena.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/12_hyena.ipynb`](../notebooks/12_hyena.ipynb)

Implicit long convolutions (filters generated by a small MLP) combined with multiplicative gating; a sub-quadratic attention replacement.

### 13. RWKV
*Reference: Peng et al. 2023*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/rwkv.py`](../llmseries/models/rwkv.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/13_rwkv.ipynb`](../notebooks/13_rwkv.ipynb)

RWKV-4 style token-shift, WKV time mixing (exponentially decaying weighted average) and channel mixing. Trains in parallel, runs as an RNN. WKV uses an exact parallel scan.

### 14. Mamba (S6)
*Reference: Gu & Dao 2023*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/mamba.py`](../llmseries/models/mamba.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/14_mamba.ipynb`](../notebooks/14_mamba.ipynb)

Selective SSM: B, C and the step size depend on the input so the model chooses what to remember. Pure PyTorch parallel scan (no CUDA kernel). No MLP blocks, 16 layers.

### 15. Mamba-2 (SSD)
*Reference: Dao & Gu 2024*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/mamba2.py`](../llmseries/models/mamba2.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/15_mamba2.ipynb`](../notebooks/15_mamba2.ipynb)

State Space Duality: a scalar decay per head makes the SSM equal to a masked attention-like product. Implemented in the quadratic (dual) form.

### 16. xLSTM (mLSTM)
*Reference: Beck et al. 2024*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/xlstm.py`](../llmseries/models/xlstm.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/16_xlstm.ipynb`](../notebooks/16_xlstm.ipynb)

mLSTM variant of xLSTM: matrix memory, exponential input gate with stabilizer, parallel form. sLSTM blocks are not included.

### 17. Griffin (RG-LRU + local attn)
*Reference: De et al. 2024*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/griffin.py`](../llmseries/models/griffin.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/17_griffin.ipynb`](../notebooks/17_griffin.ipynb)

Gated linear recurrence (RG-LRU) layers mixed with local sliding-window attention in a 2:1 ratio.

### 18. Hybrid Mamba+Attn
*Reference: Jamba 2024; Samba 2024*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/hybrid.py`](../llmseries/models/hybrid.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/18_hybrid.ipynb`](../notebooks/18_hybrid.ipynb)

Mamba layers with a full-attention layer every 4th layer plus MLPs. Tests whether a little attention repairs the recall weaknesses of pure SSMs.

### 19. MoE Transformer
*Reference: Shazeer 2017; Fedus 2021; Jiang 2024*  
Code: [`llmseries/models/moe.py`](../llmseries/models/moe.py) · Notebook: [`notebooks/19_moe.ipynb`](../notebooks/19_moe.ipynb)

Transformer whose FFN is a top-2 router over 8 SwiGLU experts with a load-balancing loss. 10M is TOTAL parameters; active parameters per token are lower (stored in the results JSON).
